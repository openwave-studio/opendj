## Getting started

This mapping is for the Party Mix MKII and the Party Mix Live. Plug one in
with its USB cable and pick **Numark Party Mix MKII**. It's also a sound
card: its output plays the main mix and the headphone jack the cue.

It's the first Party Mix's mapping with the MKII's own notes, which come
from djay Pro's built-in mapping for these two controllers. Nobody has
tried either in openDJ yet, so tell us how it goes.

- **The jog wheels** nudge; touch the top of a platter to scratch.
- **PAD MODE** steps through **CUE** (hot cues), **LOOP** (auto loops),
  **SAMPLER** and **EFFECT**. The pads also have a second set of notes
  (most likely sent with **PAD MODE** held): in CUE mode they delete that
  hot cue, in SAMPLER mode they stop that sample.
- **BROWSE** moves through the list (press it to open a folder) and
  **LOAD** loads the selected track on that side.
- **LEVEL**, **TREBLE**, **BASS**, **FILTER**, the faders, **MAIN** and
  **HEADPHONE** work in openDJ's mixer.
- The Party Mix Live's light show isn't mapped.

## When you try it

- Play, cue, sync, scratch and nudge both decks.
- Try all four pad modes, and hold PAD MODE while pressing a pad in CUE
  mode to check it deletes the hot cue.
- Turn FILTER both ways.
- Load tracks and mix with the crossfader.
