## Getting started

The RP-8000 is a turntable with eight pads for software. Connect it with
its USB cable; openDJ picks this mapping by itself. Set each turntable to
deck 1 or 2; the mapping also listens on decks 3 and 4.

This is openDJ's own mapping, built from the list of controls djay Pro's
built-in mapping uses. It covers the pads, their modes and browsing; the
platter and pitch fader play the record itself. Nobody has tried it in
openDJ yet, so tell us how it goes.

- **Pad modes:** **CUE** (hot cues; SHIFT and a pad deletes one),
  **LOOP** (loops of 1/4 to 32 beats; with **SHIFT**, loop rolls),
  **SAMPLE** (the samplers; SHIFT and a pad stops one) and **SLICER**
  (with **SHIFT**, stems: pads 1 to 4 play one stem alone, 5 to 8 turn
  one on and off).
- **Browsing:** turn the knob to move through the list and press it to
  load the track.

## When you try it

- Check it shows up in **Settings › Controllers**.
- Try each pad mode with the turntable set to deck 1, then deck 2.
