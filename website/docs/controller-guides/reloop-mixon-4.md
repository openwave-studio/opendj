## Getting started

Plug the Mixon 4 into the computer with its USB cable; openDJ picks this
mapping by itself. Play through the Mixon 4's own sound card: pick it in
**Settings › Audio**.

This is openDJ's own mapping, built from the list of controls djay Pro's
built-in mapping uses. Nobody has tried it in openDJ yet, so tell us how it
goes.

- **Four decks:** each side's deck buttons switch between decks 1 and 3 or
  2 and 4.
- **Pad modes:** the four buttons above the pads, and with **SHIFT** four
  more: hot cue, loop roll, sampler, slicer; cue loop, saved loop, pitch
  play and slicer loop. **Saved loop** pads do nothing yet.
- **FX sections:** each works on the deck you last used on its side. The
  three knobs and buttons set and turn on effects 1 to 3 (with **SHIFT**,
  the next effect); the fourth knob sets the effects' amount.
- **SHIFT** and the jog searches through the track. **SHIFT** and the
  beat jump buttons change the key.

## When you try it

- Check it shows up in **Settings › Controllers**.
- Play, cue and sync all four decks, and scratch with the jogs.
- Try each pad mode and both FX sections on decks 1 and 3.
