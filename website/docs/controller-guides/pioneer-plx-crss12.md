## Getting started

The PLX-CRSS12 sends MIDI for its performance pads, so openDJ can use them
next to the turntable:

1. Connect the PLX-CRSS12's USB port to the computer.
2. Set which deck the turntable plays (1 to 4) on the turntable.
3. openDJ picks this mapping by itself.

- **HOT CUE** plays hot cues 1 to 8 (SHIFT clears), **SAMPLER** plays
  samplers 1 to 8 (SHIFT stops), **PAD FX** plays rolls from 1/16 to 8 beats
  and **BEAT JUMP** jumps 1, 2, 4 and 8 beats back and forward.

The platter doesn't control openDJ: the PLX-CRSS12 sends its position in its
own protocol, not MIDI. Play openDJ from it with timecode vinyl (DVS)
instead.

Not mapped yet: STEMS, SAMPLE SCRATCH, the PARAMETER buttons and instant
doubles.

## When you try it

- Check the pads send on the deck you set on the turntable.
- Check the hot cue lights follow.
