## Getting started

Plug the DDJ-SZ in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by itself.
It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the main
output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4.

The SZ sends the same MIDI as the DDJ-SX2 for its decks, pads and mixer, so
it runs Mixxx's DDJ-SX mapping (see the [DDJ-SX guide](pioneer-ddj-sx.md))
with these changes:

- The two **BROWSE** knobs both move through the library; pressing one loads
  the selected track to its side's deck.
- **BACK** and **LOAD PREPARE** work as on the SX.
- The Sound Color FX buttons **ECHO**, **PITCH** and **FILTER** pick what
  the **COLOR FX** knobs play on every deck; press the lit one to go back to
  the filter. **JET**, the **OSCILLATOR** section, **USB A/B** and the
  velocity sampler do nothing yet.

## When you try it

- Play and scratch on all four decks.
- Try every pad mode, with and without SHIFT.
- Turn each channel's **COLOR FX** knob and check the filter follows.
