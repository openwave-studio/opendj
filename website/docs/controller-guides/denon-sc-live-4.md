## Getting started

The SC LIVE 4 is a standalone four-deck console. To play openDJ with it,
plug it into the computer with its USB cable, hold **MENU** to open the
Control Center and tap **Computer Mode**. openDJ then picks this mapping
by itself.

The mapping comes from a Mixxx pull request that hasn't been merged yet;
its authors wrote it on the real SC LIVE 4. Nobody has tried it in openDJ
yet, so tell us how it goes.

- **Decks:** **DECK** switches each side between decks 1 and 3 or 2 and 4.
- **The mixer:** **LEVEL**, **HIGH**, **MID**, **LOW**, **SWEEP FX** and
  the channel faders set openDJ's mixer; **CUE** sends a channel to the
  headphones and **CUE MIX** blends cue and main. The channels run 3, 1,
  2, 4 from left to right, as printed.
- **Sweep FX:** **FILTER**, **ECHO**, **NOISE** and **WASH** pick what the
  **SWEEP FX** knobs do.
- **BPM FX:** **SELECT** picks the effect, **CHANNEL ASSIGN** where it
  goes, **TIME/PARAMETER** and **AMOUNT** set it and **FX ON/OFF** turns
  it on.
- **The pads:** **HOT CUE**, **LOOP**, **ROLL** and **SLICER** pick the
  pad mode; **PARAMETER ◄ ►** change it.
- **Loops:** turn **AUTO LOOP** for the loop size and press it to loop;
  **IN** and **OUT** set a loop by hand.
- **Browsing:** turn **BROWSE** to move through the list and press
  **LOAD ◄** or **LOAD ►** to load a deck.

The pad meters and the flashing at the end of a track need values openDJ
doesn't report yet, so they stay dark.

## When you try it

- Switch each side to decks 3 and 4 and back.
- Try each pad mode, and an auto loop.
- Pick a BPM effect and assign it to a channel.
