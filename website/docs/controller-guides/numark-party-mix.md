## Getting started

Plug the Party Mix in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by
itself. It's also a sound card: its output plays the main mix and the
headphone jack the cue.

The mapping is Mixxx's, written for the first Party Mix. The Party Mix
MKII and Party Mix Live send different notes: pick
[Numark Party Mix MKII](numark-party-mix-mkii.md) for those. Nobody has
tried the Party Mix in openDJ yet, so tell us how it goes.

- **The jog wheels** nudge; with **SCRATCH** lit they scratch.
- **PAD MODE** steps through the four pad modes shown by the lights
  beside it: **CUE** (hot cues), **LOOP** (auto loops), **SAMPLER** and
  **EFFECT**. Its notes match djay Pro's built-in Party Mix mapping.
- **BROWSE** moves through the list (press it to open a folder) and
  **LOAD** loads the selected track on that side.
- **GAIN**, **TREBLE**, **BASS**, the faders, **MASTER GAIN**, **CUE MIX**
  and **CUE GAIN** work in openDJ's mixer.

## When you try it

- Play, cue, sync, scratch and nudge both decks.
- Try all four pad modes.
- Load tracks and mix with the crossfader.
