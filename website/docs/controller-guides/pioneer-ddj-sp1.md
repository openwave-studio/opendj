## Getting started

The DDJ-SP1 is an add-on: plug it in beside your main controller and it
picks this mapping by itself. It has no sound card.

It runs Mixxx's DDJ-SX script (see the [DDJ-SX guide](pioneer-ddj-sx.md))
moved to the SP1's messages from Pioneer's MIDI message list: the pads,
effects, loops and SYNC for decks 1-4. **NEEDLE SEARCH** and turning
**AUTO LOOP** do nothing yet.

## When you try it

- Switch between decks 1-4 and play hot cues on each.
- Try every pad mode, with and without SHIFT.
- Turn the FX knobs and check the effects on screen.
