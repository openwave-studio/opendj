## Getting started

The MC7000 is a four-channel controller with two USB ports. Plug it in
with a USB cable and openDJ picks this mapping by itself; it's also the
sound card. Set a channel's input selector to the USB port your computer
uses to play an openDJ deck through it.

The mapping is Mixxx's. Nobody has tried it in openDJ on a real MC7000
yet, so tell us how it goes.

- **Decks:** **DECK 1/3** and **DECK 2/4** switch each side between its
  two decks.
- **The mixer:** **LEVEL**, **HI**, **MID**, **LOW**, **FILTER** and the
  channel faders set openDJ's mixer. **CUE** sends a channel to the
  headphones; **A / THRU / B** and **X FADER CONTOUR** set the crossfader.
- **The pads:** **CUE**, **ROLL**, **SLICER** and **SAMPLER** pick the
  pad mode; hold **SHIFT** for the second four.
- **NEEDLE DROP:** touch the strip to jump through the track.
- **Effects:** **FX 1** to **FX 3** turn effects on and their knobs set
  them; **ASSIGN** sends a channel to an effect unit.
- **SLIP**, **VINYL**, **KEY LOCK** and **STOP TIME** change how the deck
  plays.

## When you try it

- Switch each side to decks 3 and 4 and back.
- Try each pad mode, with and without **SHIFT**.
- Drag a finger along **NEEDLE DROP** while a track plays.
