## Getting started

Plug the Mixtour Pro into the computer with its USB cable; openDJ picks
this mapping by itself. Play through the Mixtour Pro's own sound card: pick
it in **Settings › Audio**.

This is openDJ's own mapping, written from the MIDI notes djay Pro's
built-in mapping uses. Nobody has tried it in openDJ yet, so tell us how it
goes.

- **Four decks:** **SHIFT** and **LOAD** switch the left side between decks
  1 and 3 and the right side between decks 2 and 4.
- **The pads** follow the side whose **MODE** button you pressed last
  (**SPLIT** gives each side four of them). Hold **MODE** and press a pad to
  pick its mode, as printed under the pads:
  - **HOT CUE**: SHIFT and a pad deletes the hot cue.
  - **BOUNCE LOOP**: loop rolls of 1/16 to 8 beats while you hold the pad.
  - **PITCH CUE**: the hot cue played in its own key and up to three
    semitones higher (left column) or one to four lower (right column);
    SHIFT and a pad picks the hot cue.
  - **INSTANT FX**: pads 1 to 3 turn the deck's effects on and off, pad 4
    the whole effect unit, pads 5 to 7 pick the next effect.
  - **AUTO LOOP**: loops of 1/4 to 32 beats.
  - **SAMPLER**: a pad plays a sample, SHIFT and the pad stops it.
  - **SAVED LOOPS**: beat jumps here, back (left column) or forward (right
    column) by 1, 2, 4 or 8 beats.
  - **NEURAL MIX**: the left column mutes a stem (drums, bass, melody,
    vocals), the right column plays one alone.
- **STEMS EQ** turns **HIGH**, **MID** and **LOW** into the vocals, melody
  and drums volumes; press it again for the EQ.
- **IN-OUT-EX** sets the loop's start, then its end, then leaves the loop;
  with **SHIFT** it's an auto loop. **SHIFT** and **SYNC** matches the key.
  **SHIFT** and **CUE** or **PLAY** bends the tempo. Hold **MODE** for
  **KEY −/+** (CUE/PLAY) and to halve or double the loop (IN-OUT-EX/SYNC).
- **FX paddles:** up turns the deck's effects on, down while you hold it.
  **SHIFT** and a paddle spins the track back. **DRY/WET** sets every
  deck's effects; the arrows change how much of them you hear, and with
  **SHIFT** pick the previous or next effect.
- Turn **BROWSE** to move through the list and press it to open a folder;
  with **SHIFT**, the sidebar and back. Hold **MODE** and turn **BROWSE**
  to move through the track a beat at a time.

The **CFX** buttons and fader start aren't mapped.

## When you try it

- Check it shows up in **Settings › Controllers**.
- Play, cue and sync all four decks.
- Try each pad mode and **STEMS EQ**.
- Try the FX paddles, both ways, and **DRY/WET**.
