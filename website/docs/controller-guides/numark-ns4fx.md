## Getting started

Plug the NS4FX in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by itself.
It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the main
output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4. Its microphone is mixed
in the hardware, so openDJ can't record it.

The mapping is Mixxx's, with fixes from openDJ. Nobody has tried it in
openDJ on a real NS4FX yet, so tell us how it goes.

- **Four decks:** **LAYER** switches the left side between decks 1 and 3
  and the right side between decks 2 and 4. The four mixer channels are
  laid out 3, 1, 2, 4, as printed under the faders.
- **The jog wheels** scratch when **SCRATCH** is lit and nudge when it
  isn't; SHIFT and the wheel searches through the track. The screens show
  the deck's BPM, time and pitch.
- **Pad modes:** **CUE** (hot cues 1-4, with 5-8 on SHIFT), **AUTO LOOP**,
  **FADER CUTS** and **SAMPLE**. Press a mode button again for its second
  mode: **ROLL** and **SLICER** work; Pitch Play keeps the hot cues and
  Scratch Banks keeps the sampler for now. The bottom row plays stutter,
  back to start, rewind and fast forward.
- **Loops:** **LOOP** turns an auto loop on or off (SHIFT: reloop), **1/2**
  and **X2** halve and double it, and with SHIFT set the loop in and out.
  The right side sends those two the other way round, and the mapping
  follows it, as djay Pro's built-in mapping does.
- **Effects:** the six buttons pick the effect, **FX WET/DRY** and
  **BEATS** set it, and each side's paddle turns it on (up latches, down
  holds). **TAP** taps the tempo.
- The crossfader assign switches on the front set each channel's side.

## When you try it

- Play, scratch and nudge all four decks with **LAYER**.
- Try every pad mode, and each mode button's second press.
- Set a loop with SHIFT + **1/2** and SHIFT + **X2** on both sides, and
  check which one sets the loop in on the right.
- Pick effects and turn them on with both paddles.
