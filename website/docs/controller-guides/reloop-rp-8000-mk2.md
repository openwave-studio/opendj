## Getting started

The RP-8000 MK2 is a turntable with eight pads for software. Connect it
with its USB cable; openDJ picks this mapping by itself. Set each
turntable to deck 1 or 2; the mapping also listens on decks 3 and 4.

This is openDJ's own mapping, built from the list of controls djay Pro's
built-in mapping uses. It covers the pads, their modes and browsing; the
platter and pitch fader play the record itself. Nobody has tried it in
openDJ yet, so tell us how it goes.

- **Pad modes:** the four buttons above the pads pick hot cue, loop,
  sampler and slicer; with **SHIFT**, saved loop, bounce loop, pitch play
  and stems. Saved Loop pads do nothing yet.
  - **HOT CUE**: SHIFT and a pad deletes the hot cue.
  - **STEMS**: pads 1 to 4 turn a stem on and off, 5 to 8 play one alone.
- **Browsing:** turn the knob to move through the list and press it to
  load the track to the turntable's deck.

## When you try it

- Check it shows up in **Settings › Controllers**.
- Try each pad mode with the turntable set to deck 1, then deck 2.
