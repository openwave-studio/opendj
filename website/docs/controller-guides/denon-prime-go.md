## Getting started

The PRIME GO is a portable standalone two-deck console. To play openDJ
with it, plug it into the computer with its USB cable, hold **VIEW** to
open the Control Center and tap **Computer Mode**. openDJ then picks this
mapping by itself.

The mapping comes from a Mixxx pull request that's still a work in
progress. Nobody has tried it in openDJ yet, so tell us how it goes.

- **The mixer:** **LEVEL**, **HIGH**, **MID**, **LOW**, the **SWEEP**
  knobs and the channel faders set openDJ's mixer; **CUE** sends a channel
  to the headphones. Sweep FX button **1** turns the sweep on.
- **The pads:** **HOT CUE**, **LOOP** and **ROLL** pick the pad mode;
  **BANK** switches the four pads between 1 to 4 and 5 to 8.
- **Browsing:** turn **BROWSE** to move through the list and press
  **LOAD** to load a deck.

The BPM effects, the **AUTO LOOP** knob, Sweep FX button **2** and the
microphones aren't mapped yet.

## When you try it

- Play two decks and mix with the crossfader.
- Try each pad mode, with both banks.
