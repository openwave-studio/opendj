## Getting started

1. Connect the DJM-T1's USB port to the computer.
2. In **Settings › Audio**, choose the DJM-T1 as the sound card, set
   **Mixing** to **External mixer**, and send Deck 1 and Deck 2 to its USB
   channels.
3. openDJ picks this mapping by itself.

This mapping is openDJ's own, written from the DJM-T1's MIDI messages. The
DJM-T1 mixes the sound itself; openDJ follows its channel faders and
crossfader.

- **PLAY/PAUSE**, **CUE** (SHIFT: back to the start), **SYNC** (SHIFT: make
  this the deck the others sync to) and **QUANTIZE**.
- The **LOOP** knob halves and doubles the loop and sets a 4-beat loop when
  pressed; SHIFT and turn moves the loop.
- **Pads** play hot cues 1 to 4 (SHIFT clears) or samplers 1 to 4 (SHIFT
  stops).
- The two **FX** units: each knob sets an effect's amount, its button turns
  it on, and **FX1/FX2 ASSIGN** put the units on each deck.
- **BROWSE** moves through the list and opens folders; SHIFT and press goes
  back. **LOAD** loads the selected track.

Not mapped yet: the FX BEAT knobs, TAP, FX MODE, RE-TRIGGER, the sampler
volume, sync and master buttons, and the sort buttons.

## When you try it

- Check the LOOP knob turns the right way.
- Check the FX ASSIGN lights follow.
