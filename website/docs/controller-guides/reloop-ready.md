## Getting started

Plug the Ready into the computer with its USB cable; openDJ picks this
mapping by itself. Play through the Ready's own sound card: pick it in
**Settings › Audio**.

This is openDJ's own mapping, built from the list of controls djay Pro's
built-in mapping uses. Nobody has tried it in openDJ yet, so tell us how it
goes.

- **Pad modes:** hold **MODE** and press a pad: hot cue, auto loop, manual
  loop, sampler, pitch play, instant FX, bounce loop or slicer, in the
  order printed under the pads. Each deck keeps its own mode.
  - **HOT CUE**: SHIFT and a pad deletes the hot cue.
  - **AUTO LOOP**: loops of 1/4 to 32 beats.
  - **MANUAL LOOP**: loop in, loop out, halve, double, loop on/off and
    reloop.
  - **PITCH PLAY**: the pads play the hot cue at other keys; SHIFT and a
    pad picks the hot cue.
  - **INSTANT FX**: pads 1 to 3 turn the deck's effects on and off, pad 4
    the whole effect unit, pads 5 to 7 pick the next effect.
  - **BOUNCE LOOP**: loop rolls while you hold the pad.
  - **SLICER**: the pads play the eight beats of the section, in time.
- **FX UNIT:** the three buttons turn the deck's effects on and off (with
  **SHIFT**, the next effect); **LEVEL** sets how much you hear.
- **LOOP:** turn to halve or double the loop, press to start or leave it.

## When you try it

- Check it shows up in **Settings › Controllers**.
- Play, cue and sync both decks, and scratch with the jog.
- Try each pad mode, and check the pads light in each mode's colour.
