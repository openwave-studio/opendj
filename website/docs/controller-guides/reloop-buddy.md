## Getting started

Plug the Buddy into the computer with its USB cable; openDJ picks this
mapping by itself. Play through the Buddy's own sound card: pick it in
**Settings › Audio**.

This is openDJ's own mapping, built from the list of controls djay Pro's
built-in mapping uses. Nobody has tried it in openDJ yet, so tell us how it
goes.

- **Pad modes:** hold **MODE** and press a pad: hot cue, auto loop, instant
  FX, sampler, slicer, bounce loop, neural mix or looper, in the order
  printed under the pads. Each deck keeps its own mode.
  - **HOT CUE**: SHIFT and a pad deletes the hot cue.
  - **NEURAL MIX**: the top row mutes a stem, the bottom row plays one
    alone.
  - **LOOPER** isn't mapped yet.
- **Stems:** **SHIFT** and the deck's headphone button turns **HIGH**,
  **LOW** and **FILTER** into the vocals, instruments and drums levels;
  again for the EQ.
- **FX:** the paddle turns the deck's effects on, or on while you hold it.
  The knob sets how much you hear; the arrows change the effect's amount,
  and with **SHIFT** pick the previous or next effect.
- **SHIFT** and the jog searches through the track.

## When you try it

- Check it shows up in **Settings › Controllers**.
- Play, cue and sync both decks, and scratch with the jog.
- Try each pad mode and the stems knobs.
