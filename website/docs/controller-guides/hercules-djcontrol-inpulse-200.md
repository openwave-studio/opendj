## Getting started

Plug the Inpulse 200 in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by
itself. It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the
main output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4. Its **MASTER** and
**HEADPHONES** knobs work on the controller's sound card directly.

The mapping is Mixxx's, checked against Hercules' MIDI command list. Nobody
has tried it in openDJ yet, so tell us how it goes.

- **VINYL** turns scratching on the jog wheels on or off. Hold **SHIFT** and
  turn a wheel to search through the track.
- **LOOP IN** sets a 4-beat loop and **LOOP OUT** turns it off; with
  **SHIFT** they halve and double it.
- The two mode buttons pick the pad mode: **HOT CUE** and **ROLL** with a
  short press, **FX** and **SAMPLER** with a long one.
  - **HOT CUE**: SHIFT and a pad deletes the hot cue.
  - **ROLL**: loop rolls of 1, 2, 4 and 8 beats.
  - **FX**: pads 1-3 turn the deck's effects on, pad 4 the whole unit.
  - **SAMPLER**: a pad plays a sample, SHIFT and the pad stops it.
- **ASSISTANT** turns Automix on or off. Press **BROWSER** to switch between
  the sidebar and the track list.

## When you try it

- Play, cue, sync and scratch both decks.
- Try all four pad modes, with and without SHIFT.
- Load tracks and mix with the crossfader.
