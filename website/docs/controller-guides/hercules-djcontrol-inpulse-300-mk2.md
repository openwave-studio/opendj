## Getting started

The Inpulse 300 MK2 tells your computer it's an Inpulse 300, so openDJ
first gives it the Inpulse 300 mapping. In **Settings › Controllers**, pick
**Hercules DJControl Inpulse 300 MK2** for it instead. It's also a sound
card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the main output on channels 1-2
and the headphones on 3-4. Its **MASTER** and **HEADPHONES** knobs work on
the controller's sound card directly.

The MK2 sends the same MIDI notes as the first Inpulse 300, so this mapping
is the Inpulse 300 one with the MK2's new buttons. Nobody has tried it in
openDJ yet, so tell us how it goes.

- **LOOP ON** turns a loop on or off. **IN** and **OUT** set a loop; with
  SHIFT they halve and double it.
- **SHIFT** and **SLIP** turns scratching on the jog wheels on or off (SLIP
  lights up while you hold SHIFT when it's on).
- **Q** turns quantize on or off; SHIFT and **Q** steps the tempo fader's
  range through 8 %, 16 % and 50 %.
- The FX section above each jog wheel: **FX ON** turns the effect on (SHIFT
  and **FX ON** picks the next one), **DEPTH** sets the mix and **BEAT** the
  effect's amount.
- The four mode buttons pick the pad mode: **HOT CUE**, **LOOP** (loop
  rolls), **SLICER** and **SAMPLER**, and with SHIFT **TONEPLAY**, **FX**,
  **SLICER LOOP** and **BEATJUMP**.
- **ASSISTANT** turns Automix on or off.

## When you try it

- Play, cue, sync and scratch both decks.
- Set a loop with IN and OUT, resize it, and leave it with LOOP ON.
- Try all eight pad modes, with and without SHIFT.
- Check the DEPTH and BEAT knobs and the pad lights.
