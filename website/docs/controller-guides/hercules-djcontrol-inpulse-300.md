## Getting started

Plug the Inpulse 300 in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by
itself. It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the
main output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4. Its **MASTER** and
**HEADPHONES** knobs work on the controller's sound card directly.

The mapping is Mixxx's, checked against Hercules' MIDI command list. Nobody
has tried it in openDJ yet, so tell us how it goes.

- The four mode buttons pick the pad mode: **HOT CUE**, **ROLL**,
  **SLICER** and **SAMPLER** with a short press, **TONEPLAY**, **FX**,
  **SLICER LOOP** and **BEATJUMP** with a long one.
- The FX section above each jog wheel: **ON** turns the effect on (SHIFT
  and **ON** picks the next one), **FX** sets its amount and **DRY/WET** the
  mix.
- **SLIP** and **Q** (quantize) sit beside the jog wheel. SHIFT and **Q**
  moves the beat grid to the play head.
- **IN** and **OUT** set a loop; with SHIFT they halve and double it.
- **ASSISTANT** turns Automix on or off.

## When you try it

- Play, cue, sync and scratch both decks.
- Try all eight pad modes, with and without SHIFT.
- Check the effect knobs and the pad lights.
