## Getting started

The Mix Ultra talks to computers over Bluetooth MIDI. Switch it on, then
pair it: on a Mac open **Audio MIDI Setup › Window › Show MIDI Studio**, click
the Bluetooth button and connect **DJControl Mix Ultra**; on Windows and
Linux pair it in the system's Bluetooth settings. openDJ then picks this
mapping by itself. The Mix Ultra has no sound card, so play through your
computer's audio.

This is openDJ's own mapping, written from the user manual and the MIDI notes
djay Pro's built-in mapping uses. Nobody has tried it in openDJ yet, so tell
us how it goes.

- **Jog wheels**: the top scratches, the ring nudges the tempo. With
  **SHIFT** either one moves quickly through the track.
- **SHIFT** and **CUE** plays from the start. **SHIFT** and **HIGH** sets
  the gain.
- The four mode buttons pick the pad mode, and with SHIFT a second one:
  - **HOT CUE** (SHIFT: pitch play, not mapped yet): SHIFT and a pad deletes
    the hot cue.
  - **LOOP**: loops of 1/4 to 32 beats. SHIFT and **LOOP** picks **BOUNCE
    LOOP**: loop rolls of 1/32 to 4 beats while you hold the pad.
  - **FX**: pads 1-3 turn the deck's effects on, pad 4 the whole unit, pads
    5-7 pick the next effect (SHIFT: slicer, not mapped yet).
  - **NEURAL MIX**: the top pads play one stem alone (drums, bass, melody,
    vocals), the bottom pads mute one. SHIFT and **NEURAL MIX** picks
    **SAMPLER**: a pad plays a sample, SHIFT and the pad stops it.
- The **NEURAL MIX** button in the mixer turns **HIGH**, **MID** and
  **LOW** into the vocals, melody and drums volumes; press it again for the
  EQ.
- Press **BROWSER** to open a folder, SHIFT and **BROWSER** to go back.

## When you try it

- Pair it and check it shows up in **Settings › Controllers**.
- Play, cue, sync and scratch both decks.
- Try the pad modes, and the NEURAL MIX button with the EQ knobs.
- Load tracks and mix with the crossfader.
