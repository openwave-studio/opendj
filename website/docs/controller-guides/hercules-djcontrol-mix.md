## Getting started

Connect the DJControl Mix with its USB-C cable and it picks this mapping by
itself. The mapping is Mixxx's; Bluetooth isn't used. Nobody has tried it in
openDJ yet, so tell us how it goes.

- **VINYL** turns scratching on the jog wheels on or off. Hold **SHIFT** and
  turn a wheel to search through the track.
- The lower knob of each channel is **FILTER**; the **FILTER/BASS** button
  between the knobs switches it to **BASS** on the controller.
- The two buttons above the pads pick the mode: **HOT CUE** and **SAMPLER**;
  with SHIFT, **FX** and **LOOP**. SHIFT and a pad deletes a hot cue, or
  changes the effect in FX mode.
- The headphone buttons put each deck in the headphones. With SHIFT, the
  deck 1 button sends the main mix to the headphones and the deck 2 button
  mixes cue and main.
- **MASTER** and **HEADPHONES** set openDJ's main and headphone volume.

## When you try it

- Play, cue, sync and scratch both decks.
- Try all four pad modes.
- Mix with the volume knobs and the crossfader.
