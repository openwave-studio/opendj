## Getting started

The DN-HS5500 is a media player with a spinning platter. Put it in MIDI
mode and plug it in with its USB cable; openDJ picks this mapping by
itself. Two units play decks 1 and 2.

The mapping is Mixxx's. Nobody has tried it in openDJ on a real
DN-HS5500 yet, so tell us how it goes.

- **Playing:** **PLAY/PAUSE**, **CUE**, the platter and the **PITCH**
  fader with **PITCH BEND +** and **−**. **BRAKE** and **REVERSE**
  change how the deck stops and plays.
- **Hot cues and loops:** **A1**, **A2** and **B** are hot cues 1 to 3.
  **A1** and **B** also set the loop's start and end, and
  **EXIT/RELOOP** leaves or returns to it.
- **Searching:** **FAST SEARCH** moves through the track and **TRACK
  SELECT** through the list.
- **KEY ADJUST** turns key lock on or off.

## When you try it

- Play and scratch with the platter.
- Set a loop with **A1** and **B**, then leave and return to it.
