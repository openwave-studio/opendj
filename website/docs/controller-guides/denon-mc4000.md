## Getting started

The MC4000 is a two-channel controller and mixer. Plug it in with its USB
cable and openDJ picks this mapping by itself; it's also the sound card.

The mapping is Mixxx's, with one fix: **PITCH BEND +** now nudges the
tempo up. Before, it listened to the **KEY LOCK** button instead. Nobody
has tried it in openDJ on a real MC4000 yet, so tell us how it goes.

- **The mixer:** **LEVEL**, **HI**, **MID**, **LOW**, **FILTER** and the
  channel faders set openDJ's mixer. **CUE** sends a channel to the
  headphones; the front panel sets the headphone level, the cue mix and
  **SPLIT CUE**.
- **Hot cues:** **HOT CUE 1** to **HOT CUE 4**.
- **Loops:** **AUTO** sets a four-beat loop and **RELOOP** leaves or
  returns to it.
- **Sampler:** **SAMPLER 1** to **SAMPLER 4** play the samplers (with
  **SHIFT**, stop them) and the **SAMPLER** knob sets their level.
- **Effects:** **FX 1** to **FX 3** turn effects on, **BEATS** sets the
  timing and **TAP** the tempo.
- **Browsing:** turn **LIBRARY** to move through the list, **LOAD 1** and
  **LOAD 2** load a deck and **LOAD PREP** adds to the prepare list.

## When you try it

- Press **PITCH BEND +** and **PITCH BEND −** while a track plays.
- Try the hot cues, **AUTO** and **RELOOP**.
- Cue a deck in the headphones with **SPLIT CUE** on and off.
