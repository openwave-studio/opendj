## Getting started

The XDJ-XZ is an all-in-one system, so openDJ drives it in its **PC
control** (MIDI) mode:

1. Connect the XDJ-XZ to the computer with a USB cable and switch its decks to PC control the way you would for rekordbox (**BROWSE**, then **MIDI**, then **LOAD**).
2. In **Settings › Audio**, choose the XDJ-XZ as the sound card.
3. openDJ picks this mapping by itself.

Use the **4-Deck** layout to see all four decks on screen.

This mapping is openDJ's own, written from AlphaTheta's list of the XDJ-XZ's MIDI messages and the messages rekordbox maps on it. It shares its
script with openDJ's other Pioneer all-in-one mappings, so they work the same
way where they share controls. The picture in the Controller Editor is a
sketch from the unit's size, not measured.

- **PLAY/PAUSE**, **CUE** (SHIFT: back to the start), the jog wheel
  (**VINYL** swaps scratching and bending; SHIFT and turn searches) and the
  **TEMPO** slider work on each deck. **MASTER TEMPO** is key lock, **BEAT
  SYNC** syncs (held or with SHIFT: this deck leads) and **QUANTIZE** sets
  quantize for every deck.
- **LOOP IN**, **LOOP OUT** and **RELOOP/EXIT** set and leave loops; holding
  **LOOP IN** lets the jog move the loop start. **CALL ◀ ▶** halve and double
  the loop playing.
- **TRACK SEARCH** jumps 32 beats and **SEARCH** by the deck's beat jump size.
- **Pads**: HOT CUE plays hot cues A to H (SHIFT clears them), BEAT JUMP jumps 1, 2, 4 and 8 beats each way (SHIFT: 16 times as far), PAD FX plays loop rolls and SAMPLER plays openDJ's samplers.
- **4 BEAT LOOP** sets a 4-beat loop. **TEMPO RESET** puts the tempo back to 0.
- The **ROTARY SELECTOR** moves through the list (SHIFT: zooms the
  waveforms), press opens or loads. **BACK** moves to the sidebar and **TAG
  TRACK** adds the track to Automix. **LOAD** loads the selected track.

Not mapped yet: KEYBOARD, KEY SHIFT and PAD FX2 pads, MEMORY, DELETE, TIME MODE, TRACK FILTER and VINYL SPEED ADJUST.

## When you try it

- Load a track on each deck and check PLAY, CUE, the hot cues and the jog
  wheel follow.
- Move every knob and fader, and turn on each effect.
- rekordbox leaves the XDJ-XZ's mixer and Beat FX to the unit, and so does this mapping: only the crossfader, the channel faders and the crossfader assign move openDJ's on-screen mixer. In **Settings › Audio › Mixing**, choose **External mixer** and send decks 1 to 4 to the XDJ-XZ's four computer channels. Tell us how that sounds.
- Watch the jog displays and the LOAD lights while a track plays.
