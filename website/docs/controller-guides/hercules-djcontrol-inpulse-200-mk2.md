## Getting started

Plug the Inpulse 200 MK2 in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by
itself. It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the
main output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4. Its **MASTER** and
**HEADPHONES** knobs work on the controller's sound card directly.

The MK2 sends the same MIDI notes as the first Inpulse 200, so this mapping
is the Inpulse 200 one with the MK2's new buttons. Nobody has tried it in
openDJ yet, so tell us how it goes.

- **LOOP** turns a loop on or off; **1/2** and **X2** halve and double it.
  With **SHIFT**, **1/2** and **X2** set the loop's start and end.
- **SHIFT** and **LOOP** turns scratching on the jog wheels on or off. Hold
  **SHIFT** and turn a wheel to search through the track.
- **HOT CUE** and **STEMS** pick the pad mode; with **SHIFT** they pick
  **FX** and **SAMPLER**.
  - **HOT CUE**: SHIFT and a pad deletes the hot cue.
  - **STEMS**: a pad mutes or unmutes one stem (drums, bass, melody, vocals).
    SHIFT and a pad plays that stem alone, or all four again.
  - **FX**: pads 1-3 turn the deck's effects on, pad 4 the whole unit.
  - **SAMPLER**: a pad plays a sample, SHIFT and the pad stops it.
- **ASSIST./PREP.** turns Automix on or off. Press **BROWSER** to switch
  between the sidebar and the track list.

## When you try it

- Play, cue, sync and scratch both decks.
- Set a loop with LOOP, change its size, then set one with SHIFT + 1/2 and X2.
- Try the stems pads on a track with stems, with and without SHIFT.
- Load tracks and mix with the crossfader.
