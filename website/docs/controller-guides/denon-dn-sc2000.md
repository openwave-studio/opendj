## Getting started

The DN-SC2000 is a single-deck controller: one unit plays one deck.
Plug it in with its USB cable and openDJ picks this mapping by itself.
It has no sound card, so use the computer's or another one.

The mapping is Mixxx's. Nobody has tried it in openDJ on a real
DN-SC2000 yet, so tell us how it goes.

- **Decks:** **DECK CHG.** switches between decks 1 and 2.
- **Playing:** **PLAY/PAUSE**, **CUE**, **SYNC**, **KEY LOCK**, the jog
  wheel and the **PITCH** fader with **PITCH BEND +** and **−**.
- **Hot cues:** **CUE 1** to **CUE 4**, and 5 to 8 with **SHIFT**.
- **Loops:** **LOOP IN**, **LOOP OUT** and **AUTO LOOP**. The **FILTER**
  knob sets the loop size.
- **Effects:** **FX ON 1** and the **PARAM.** knobs run the flanger.
- **Browsing:** turn **TRACK SELECT** to move through the list.

## When you try it

- Switch between decks 1 and 2 and play each.
- Turn **FILTER** to change the loop size while a loop plays.
