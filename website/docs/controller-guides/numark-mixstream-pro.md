## Getting started

The Mixstream Pro, Mixstream Pro+ and Mixstream Pro Go send the same
MIDI, so this mapping is for all three. They play on their own until you
switch them to **Computer Mode**: press **Control Center** (the gear),
tap **Source**, pick the computer icon at the top and confirm. The unit
restarts in Computer Mode; then plug in the USB cable and openDJ picks
this mapping by itself. It's also a sound card.

The mapping is Mixxx's. Nobody has tried it in openDJ on a real Mixstream
yet, so tell us how it goes.

- **The platters** are touch sensitive; **SCRATCH MODE** switches between
  scratching and nudging.
- **Pad modes:** **CUE**, **SAVED LOOP**, **AUTO LOOP** and **ROLL**;
  press a mode again for its second bank.
- **The effects:** the four **FX SELECT** buttons pick an effect and each
  channel's **EFFECTS TOGGLE** turns it on (away from you latches, towards
  you holds).
- **BROWSE**, **BACK**, **VIEW** and **LOAD 1 / 2** move through the
  library and load tracks.
- The touchscreen, lights and speakers stay the Mixstream's own: openDJ
  doesn't drive them.

## When you try it

- Play, cue, sync and scratch both decks.
- Try every pad mode and both banks.
- Pick effects and turn them on with both toggles.
- Browse and load with the knob and buttons.
