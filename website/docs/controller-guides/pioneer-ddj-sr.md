## Getting started

Plug the DDJ-SR in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by itself.
It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the main
output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4.

The DDJ-SR works like the DDJ-SX on fewer controls, so it runs Mixxx's
DDJ-SX script (see the [DDJ-SX guide](pioneer-ddj-sx.md)) moved to the
SR's messages from Pioneer's MIDI message list. Four decks; the pads have
Hot Cue, Roll, Slicer and Sampler modes. **NEEDLE SEARCH** does nothing yet.

## When you try it

- Play and scratch on all four decks.
- Try every pad mode, with and without SHIFT.
- Check that the lights follow what's on screen on decks 1-4.
