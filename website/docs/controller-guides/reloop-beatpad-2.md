## Getting started

Plug the Beatpad 2 into the computer with its USB cable; openDJ picks this
mapping by itself. Play through its own sound card: pick it in
**Settings › Audio**.

This is openDJ's own mapping, built from the list of controls djay Pro's
built-in mapping uses. Nobody has tried it in openDJ yet, so tell us how it
goes.

- **Pad modes:** the four buttons above the pads pick **HOT CUE**,
  **AUTO LOOP**, **TOUCH FX** and **SLICER**; with **SHIFT** they pick
  sampler, bounce loop and slicer loop instead. Each deck keeps its own
  mode.
  - **HOT CUE**: SHIFT and a pad deletes the hot cue.
  - **SAMPLER**: the pads play the samplers.
- **FX:** the knob sets how much effect you hear; with **SHIFT** it sets
  dry/wet.
- **Pitch:** **PITCH BEND −** and **+** nudge the tempo, **KEY LOCK** and
  **KEY MATCH** sit below them.
- **Transport:** **SYNC**, **SET** (cue), **JUMP** (back to the start with
  **SHIFT**) and **PLAY** (with **SHIFT**, a brake stop).
- **Browsing:** turn the browse knob to move through the list, press it to
  open, and **LOAD** loads a deck.

## When you try it

- Check it shows up in **Settings › Controllers**.
- Play, cue and sync both decks, and scratch with the jog.
- Try each pad mode, and check the pads light in each mode's colour.
