## Getting started

The PRIME 4 is a standalone four-deck console. To play openDJ with it,
plug it into the computer with its USB cable, hold **VIEW** to open the
Control Center and tap **Computer Mode**. openDJ then picks this mapping
by itself.

The mapping comes from a Mixxx pull request that's still a work in
progress. Nobody has tried it in openDJ yet, so tell us how it goes.

- **Decks:** the **DECK** buttons on each side pick decks 1 and 3 or 2
  and 4.
- **The mixer:** **LEVEL**, **HIGH**, **MID**, **LOW**, **SWEEP FX** and
  the channel faders set openDJ's mixer. **CUE** sends a channel to the
  headphones, and **1** and **2** above each channel send it to an effect
  unit. The channels run 3, 1, 2, 4 from left to right.
- **Sweep FX:** **FILTER**, **ECHO**, **WASH** and **NOISE** pick what the
  **SWEEP FX** knobs do.
- **Effects:** **FX 1** on the left and **FX 2** on the right are openDJ's
  two effect units: the first three knobs and the buttons under them run
  its three effects, and the fourth knob sets dry/wet.
- **The pads:** **HOT CUE**, **LOOP**, **ROLL** and **SLICER** pick the
  pad mode.
- **Beat grid:** hold **EDIT GRID** and use the two grid buttons to move
  the downbeat.

The right deck's **VINYL** button isn't mapped yet.

## When you try it

- Pick decks 3 and 4 with the **DECK** buttons.
- Send a channel to an effect unit and turn on an effect.
- Try each pad mode.
