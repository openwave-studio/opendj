## Getting started

1. Connect the DDJ-1000 to the computer with a USB cable.
2. In **Settings › Audio**, choose the DDJ-1000 as the sound card, with the
   main output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4.
3. openDJ picks this mapping by itself.

Use the **4-Deck** layout to see all four decks on screen.

This mapping is openDJ's own, written from Pioneer's list of the DDJ-1000's
MIDI messages and built on the [DDJ-FLX10 mapping](pioneer-ddj-flx10.md):
the FLX10 is the DDJ-1000's successor and sends the same messages for the
decks, pads, mixer and effects, so the two work the same way where they
share controls.

- **DECK 1/3** and **DECK 2/4** pick the deck each side plays; each deck
  sends on its own MIDI channel, and the mixer always has all four channels.
  Picking deck 3 or 4 opens the 4-Deck layout.
- **BROWSE**: turn to move, press to open the folder or playlist picked in
  the folders pane, or to load the track picked in the list onto the side's
  deck. **BACK** goes back to the folders.
- **Pads**: the pad mode buttons open the deck's pad pages (HOT CUE, PAD FX,
  BEAT JUMP, SAMPLER, and with SHIFT KEYBOARD, PAD FX 2, BEAT LOOP and KEY
  SHIFT). The pads play whatever page the deck shows on screen, and light
  in colour.
- **BEAT FX** runs openDJ's Beat FX: **BEAT FX SELECT** picks the nearest
  openDJ effect, **CH SELECT** sends it to one channel or, on MASTER, all
  four, **LEVEL/DEPTH** sets the amount, **ON/OFF** turns it on (SHIFT:
  lets the tail ring out) and **BEAT ◀ ▶** halve and double its length
  (SHIFT: AUTO and TAP tempo).
- **SOUND COLOR FX**: D.ECHO, PITCH, NOISE and FILTER pick what the COLOR
  knobs play.
- **MASTER TEMPO** turns key lock on and off (SHIFT: next tempo range).
  **KEY SYNC** matches the key of the other deck, **KEY RESET** puts the
  track back in its own key. **SHIFT + BEAT SYNC** makes the deck the one
  the others sync to. **SHIFT + CUE** on a channel taps the tempo.
- **SEARCH |◀◀** goes back to the start of the track; **▶▶|** loads the next
  track in the list while the deck is paused (while it plays, nothing
  happens). Held, they search back or forward. With SHIFT they call the
  previous and next hot cue.
- **4 BEAT/EXIT** sets a 4-beat loop or ends one; **LOOP IN** and **LOOP
  OUT** set a loop, and halve and double it while it plays; SHIFT + LOOP IN
  or LOOP OUT lets the jog move that end of the loop.
- **SLIP**, **SLIP REVERSE** and **QUANTIZE** do what they say.
- **CROSSFADER ASSIGN** puts each channel on side A, THRU or B.
- The jog displays show the playhead going round, the cue point, the BPM,
  the time played, the tempo and the key with its shift, and light MASTER
  and SYNC. The DDJ-1000 takes no waveform or artwork on them.

Not mapped yet: MEMORY and DELETE, the pad PAGE buttons, INPUT SELECT, the
mic, SAMPLER CUE, MASTER CUE and the fader start messages.

## When you try it

- Load a track on each deck, switch each side between its two decks, and
  check PLAY, CUE, the pads and the jog wheel follow.
- Move every mixer knob and fader on all four channels, and the crossfader
  assign switches.
- Try every pad mode, with and without SHIFT, and check the pad colours.
- Turn on the Beat FX, turn **BEAT FX SELECT** and **CH SELECT** through
  every position, and press each SOUND COLOR FX button.
- Watch the jog displays while a track plays: the playhead, cue point, BPM,
  time, tempo and key.
- Scratch and nudge with the platter and the rim: a full turn of the
  platter should move the track about 1.8 seconds, as a record would. If
  it's far off, tell us how far one turn moves it.
- Press DECK 1/3 and DECK 2/4 a few times and check the side always plays
  the deck its button shows.
