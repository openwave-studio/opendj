## Getting started

Plug the Mixtour into the computer with its USB cable; openDJ picks this
mapping by itself. Play through the Mixtour's own sound card: pick it in
**Settings › Audio**.

This is openDJ's own mapping, written from the MIDI notes djay Pro's
built-in mapping uses. Nobody has tried it in openDJ yet, so tell us how it
goes.

- **T** and **C** above the buttons switch each side's four buttons between
  transport (**LOOP**, **SYNC**, **CUE**, **PLAY**) and hot cues 1 to 4.
  With **SHIFT**: **LOOP** halves the loop, **SYNC** doubles it, **CUE**
  puts the beat grid's first beat here, **PLAY** goes to the start, and in
  **C** mode a button deletes its hot cue.
- **FX** turns the deck's effects on. While it's on, **FILTER** also sets
  how much of the effects you hear, more the further you turn it from the
  centre. **SHIFT** and **FX** picks the next effect; **SHIFT** and
  **FILTER** sets the effects' amount alone.
- Turn **BROWSE** to move through the list and press it to open a folder;
  **SHIFT** and **BROWSE** move through the sidebar. **BACK** goes back.
  Hold **T** and turn **BROWSE** to move through the track a beat at a
  time.
- The VU meters show what openDJ plays.

Fader start isn't mapped.

## When you try it

- Check it shows up in **Settings › Controllers**.
- Play, cue and sync both decks, in **T** mode and **C** mode.
- Turn on **FX** and try the **FILTER** knob.
- Load tracks and mix with the crossfader.
