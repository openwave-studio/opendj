## Getting started

This is for the first Beatmix (the Beatmix 2 and 4 have their own
mapping). Plug it in with its USB cable; openDJ picks this mapping by
itself.

This is openDJ's own mapping, built from the list of controls djay Pro's
built-in mapping uses. Nobody has tried it in openDJ yet, so tell us how it
goes.

- **FX:** **FX SELECT** picks the effect, **FX AMOUNT** sets how much you
  hear and **FX ON** turns it on.
- **Loops:** turn **LOOP** to halve or double the loop, press it to start
  or leave it; with **SHIFT** it jumps by beats. **AUTO LOOP** starts a
  loop; with **SHIFT** the two buttons are loop in and loop out.
- **Hot cues:** the three **HOT CUE** buttons.
- **Pitch:** **PITCH BEND −** and **+** nudge the tempo; with **SHIFT**
  they jump back and forward.
- **Transport:** **SYNC** (start with **SHIFT**), **START AND PLAY**
  (tap tempo with **SHIFT**), **CUE** and **PLAY** (key lock with
  **SHIFT**).
- **Browsing:** turn **TRAX** to move through the list and press it to
  open; **LOAD** loads a deck.

## When you try it

- Check it shows up in **Settings › Controllers**.
- Play, cue and sync both decks, and scratch with the jog.
- Try the loop knob, the hot cues and the FX section.
