## Getting started

The Elite is a two-channel battle mixer. Plug it in with its USB cable;
openDJ picks this mapping by itself. Play through its own sound card:
pick it in **Settings › Audio**.

This is openDJ's own mapping, built from the list of controls djay Pro's
built-in mapping uses. That list leaves out the channel faders, so they
aren't mapped; the crossfader is. Nobody has tried it
in openDJ yet, so tell us how it goes.

- **Pad modes:** the buttons under the pads pick **HOT CUE**,
  **LOOP ROLL**, **SLICER** and **SAMPLER**; the row above picks pitch
  play, saved loop, slicer loop and stems. Saved Loop pads do nothing yet.
  - **HOT CUE**: SHIFT and a pad deletes the hot cue.
  - **SAMPLER**: the pads play the samplers.
- **FX:** the three buttons on the left pick the next effect in each slot;
  the big knob sets dry/wet and the small one the amount. **TAP** sets the
  tempo and **KEY MATCH** matches the key.
- **Loops:** turn the loop knob to halve or double the loop and press it
  to start or leave it.
- **EQ:** **HIGH**, **MID** and **LOW** set openDJ's EQ.
- **Browsing:** turn the browse knob to move through the list, press it to
  open, and **LOAD** loads a deck.

## When you try it

- Check it shows up in **Settings › Controllers**.
- Try each pad mode on both sides.
- Load a track to each deck and turn the EQ knobs.
