## Getting started

Plug the DJ2GO2 Touch in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by
itself. It's also a sound card: its 1/8" output plays the main mix and
the headphone jack the cue.

The mapping is Mixxx's. Nobody has tried it in openDJ on a real DJ2GO2
Touch yet, so tell us how it goes.

- **The jog wheels** are touch sensitive: touch and turn to scratch, turn
  the edge to nudge.
- **PAD MODE** steps through the four pad modes, lit under the button:
  **CUES** (hot cues; hold **PAD MODE** and press a pad to delete one),
  **AUTO** (loops of 1, 2, 4 and 8 beats), **MANUAL** (loop in, loop out,
  loop on/off, reloop) and **SAMPLER**.
- **LOAD 1** and **LOAD 2** load the selected track; turn **BROWSE** to
  move through the list and press it to open a folder.
- **MASTER LEVEL**, **CUE LEVEL**, each deck's **LEVEL** and its
  headphone **CUE** button work in openDJ's mixer.

## When you try it

- Play, cue, sync and scratch both decks.
- Try all four pad modes on both decks.
- Load tracks and browse with the knob.
