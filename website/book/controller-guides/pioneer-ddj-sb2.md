## Getting started

Plug the DDJ-SB2 in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by itself.
It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**.

This is Mixxx's mapping. Four decks: **DECK 3** and **DECK 4** switch each
side. The pads have **HOT CUE**, **AUTO LOOP**, **MANUAL LOOP** and
**SAMPLER** modes, with Hot Cue 5-8, Roll, Cue Loop and Bank on SHIFT. The
**FILTER** knobs filter each deck, and the FX buttons drive openDJ's two
effect units.

## When you try it

- Play and scratch on all four decks.
- Try every pad mode, with and without SHIFT.
