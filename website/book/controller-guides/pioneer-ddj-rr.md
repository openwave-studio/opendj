## Getting started

Plug the DDJ-RR in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by itself.
It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the main
output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4.

The RR sends the same MIDI as the DDJ-RX for its decks and pads, with a
two-channel mixer, so it runs openDJ's DDJ-RX mapping (see the
[DDJ-RX guide](pioneer-ddj-rx.md)) without the RX's crossfader assign and
effect assign buttons. The **COLOR** knobs are filters. The Sound Color FX
buttons, **CAPTURE** and the sequencer do nothing yet.

## When you try it

- Play and scratch on both decks, then switch each side to decks 3 and 4.
- Try every pad mode, with and without SHIFT.
- Check that the mixer follows when a side plays deck 3 or 4.
