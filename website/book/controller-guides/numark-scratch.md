## Getting started

The Scratch is a two-channel scratch mixer. Plug it in with its USB cable
and openDJ picks this mapping by itself; it's also the sound card.
Set a channel's **PC / LINE-PHONO** switch to **PC** to play an openDJ
deck through it.

The mapping is Mixxx's. Nobody has tried it in openDJ on a real Scratch
yet, so tell us how it goes.

- **The mixer:** **GAIN**, **TREBLE**, **MID**, **BASS**, **FILTER** and
  the channel faders set openDJ's mixer. **CUE** sends a channel to the
  headphones and **CUE MIX** blends cue and main.
- **The pads:** **PAD MODE** switches between **CUE**, **SAMPLE** and
  **ROLL**; hold **SHIFT** for the second set.
- **LOOP** knobs: turn to halve or double the loop, press to turn it on.
- **Effects:** the six buttons pick an effect, **FX DRY/WET** sets it and
  each channel's paddle turns it on (up latches, down holds).
- **X-FADER REVERSE** and **SLOPE** change the crossfader.

## When you try it

- Play two decks through the mixer and cut with the crossfader.
- Try **X-FADER REVERSE** and **SLOPE**.
- Try each pad mode, with and without **SHIFT**.
