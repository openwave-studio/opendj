## Getting started

Plug the Jogvision in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by
itself. It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the
main output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4.

The mapping is Mixxx's. Nobody has tried it in openDJ yet, so tell us how
it goes.

- **FX / LOOP MODE** switches the three buttons above each jog wheel between
  effects (**FX1**-**FX3**, SHIFT picks the effect) and loops (**LOOP X½**,
  **LOOP ON**, **LOOP X2**). The encoder beside them sets the loop size or
  the effect's dry/wet; SHIFT and a turn moves the loop.
- **KEY-LOCK** turns key lock on; with SHIFT it's **SLIP**.
- Hold **MODE** and press pad 1-4 to pick the pad mode: hot cues 1-4,
  samples 1-4, hot cues 5-8 or samples 5-8. SHIFT and a pad deletes a hot
  cue.
- **AIR CONTROL**: hold a hand over the sensor to sweep the filter of a deck
  with **AIR FX** on.
- **VINYL** turns scratching on the jog wheels on or off. **VIEW** shows the
  library full size and **LOAD PREPARE** turns Automix on or off.
- SHIFT and a turn of **BROWSER** sets the deck's gain.

## When you try it

- Play, cue, sync and scratch both decks.
- Try the pad modes, the loop and effect buttons, and the air sensor.
- Check that the jog wheel lights follow the track.
