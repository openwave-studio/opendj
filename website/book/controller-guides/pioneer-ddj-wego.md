## Getting started

Plug the DDJ-WeGO in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by itself.
Choose where the sound goes in **Settings › Audio**.

AlphaTheta no longer publishes the WeGO's MIDI list, so this mapping was
written from rekordbox's list of its messages and its picture in the
Controller Editor is a sketch.

- **DECK 3** and **DECK 4** switch each side to its second deck.
- The **AUTO LOOP** knob sets a loop when pressed and halves or doubles it
  when turned. With SHIFT, turning it moves the loop and pressing it returns
  to the loop or leaves it.
- **FX1**, **FX2** and **FX3** turn that side's three effects on and off;
  with SHIFT they pick the next effect.
- Pads **1** to **4** play hot cues, or the deck's four samplers in SAMPLER
  mode; SHIFT deletes the hot cue or stops the sampler.
- The **FILTER** knobs are filters.
- The platter scratches when **Settings › Decks › Vinyl mode** is on for the
  deck; otherwise it bends the speed, as the rim does. SHIFT and the jog
  moves quickly through the track.
- The effects' **BEAT** knobs do nothing yet.

## When you try it

- Play and scratch on all four decks.
- Set a loop with AUTO LOOP and change its length.
- Try the pads in both modes, with and without SHIFT.
