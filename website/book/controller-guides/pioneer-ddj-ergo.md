## Getting started

Plug the DDJ-ERGO in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by itself.
It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the main
output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4.

AlphaTheta no longer publishes the ERGO's MIDI list, so this mapping was
written from rekordbox's list of its messages and its picture in the
Controller Editor is a sketch.

- The **DECK** switches move each side between decks 1 and 3, and 2 and 4.
- The **AUTO LOOP** knob sets a loop when pressed and halves or doubles it
  when turned; **LOOP IN** and **LOOP OUT** set a loop by hand.
- **KEY LOCK** keeps the key as the tempo changes; **VINYL** turns scratching
  on the platter on and off.
- Pads **1** to **4** play hot cues, or the deck's four samplers in SAMPLER
  mode; SHIFT deletes the hot cue or stops the sampler.
- Each side's FX section runs an effect unit: three knobs and **ON**
  buttons, with SHIFT and **ON** picking the next effect. The **FX1** and
  **FX2** buttons on each mixer channel send the channel to that unit (FX1 starts on decks 1 and 3, FX2 on decks 2 and 4).
- The **FILTER** knobs are filters. **BEATS**, **TAP**, **PREVIEW** and
  **SAMPLER VOLUME** do nothing yet.

## When you try it

- Play and scratch on all four decks.
- Set loops with AUTO LOOP and with LOOP IN and OUT.
- Turn on an effect and send a channel to it.
