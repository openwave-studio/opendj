## Getting started

Plug the DDJ-RB in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by itself.
It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the main
output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4.

The RB is laid out like the DDJ-400, so it runs openDJ's DDJ-400 mapping
(see the [DDJ-400 guide](pioneer-ddj-400.md)) moved to the RB's messages:

- The mode buttons above the pads pick **HOT CUE**, **PAD FX1**, **SLICER**
  and **SAMPLER**; with SHIFT they pick **BEAT JUMP**, **PAD FX2**,
  **SLICER LOOP** and **SEQUENCE CALL**.
- **SLICER** pads play loop rolls from 1/16 to 8 beats, and **SLICER LOOP**
  pads set beat loops.
- **BEAT JUMP** pads jump back and forward by 1, 2, 4 and 8 beats.
- **FX1** on the left works on deck 1 and **FX2** on the right on deck 2:
  **LEVEL/DEPTH** sets the amount, **BEAT ◀ ▶** halve and double the
  effect's length, **ON** turns it on and SHIFT and **ON** picks the next
  effect.
- **LOOP IN** held for a moment sets a 4-beat loop.
- **SEQUENCE CALL** does nothing yet.

## When you try it

- Play and scratch on both decks.
- Try every pad mode, with and without SHIFT.
- Check that the sampler pads blink while their sampler plays.
- Turn on both FX units.
