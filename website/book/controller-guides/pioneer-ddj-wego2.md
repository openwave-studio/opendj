## Getting started

Plug the DDJ-WeGO2 in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by
itself. Choose where the sound goes in **Settings › Audio**.

The WeGO2 sends the same MIDI as the first WeGO, so its mapping works the
same way: see the [DDJ-WeGO guide](pioneer-ddj-wego.md). AlphaTheta doesn't
publish the WeGO2's MIDI list, so this mapping was written from rekordbox's
list of its messages and its picture in the Controller Editor is a sketch.

## When you try it

- Play and scratch on all four decks.
- Set a loop with AUTO LOOP and change its length.
- Try the pads in both modes, with and without SHIFT.
