## Getting started

Plug the DDJ-FLX2 in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by itself.
It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the main
output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4.

This is openDJ's own mapping, written from AlphaTheta's MIDI message list.
The DDJ-FLX2 numbers its decks, jogs, pads and mixer like the DDJ-FLX4, so
it shares the [FLX4's mapping](pioneer-ddj-flx4.md); only its pad mode
buttons sit elsewhere:

- **Pad modes:** **HOT CUE**, **PAD FX**, **SAMPLER**, and **BEAT LOOP**
  (SHIFT and the third mode button).
- The **CFX** knobs work as filters.
- Not mapped yet: **SMART CFX** and **SMART FADER**.

## When you try it

- Play, scratch and loop on both decks.
- Try each pad mode and check its light follows.
