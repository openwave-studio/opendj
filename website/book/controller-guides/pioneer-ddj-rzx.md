## Getting started

Plug the DDJ-RZX in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by itself.
It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the main
output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4.

The RZX sends the same MIDI as the DDJ-RX for its decks, pads and mixer, so
it runs openDJ's DDJ-RX mapping (see the [DDJ-RX guide](pioneer-ddj-rx.md)).
The differences:

- Pressing a **BROWSE** knob moves to the next library pane; with SHIFT, to
  the previous one. **BACK** goes back.
- The **COLOR FX** knobs are filters.
- The screens stay dark: they show rekordbox's own pictures, which openDJ
  doesn't send. The video, touch strip and **VIEW** buttons, the Sound Color
  FX buttons, the **OSCILLATOR** section and the sequencer do nothing yet.

## When you try it

- Play and scratch on all four decks.
- Try every pad mode, with and without SHIFT.
- Move through the library with both BROWSE knobs.
