## Getting started

Plug the DDJ-REV5 in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by itself.
It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**, with the main
output on channels 1-2 and the headphones on 3-4.

This is openDJ's own mapping, written from AlphaTheta's MIDI message list.
The REV5 has the DDJ-FLX6's four decks, pads and mixer on other notes, so it
runs the [FLX6's mapping](pioneer-ddj-flx6.md) moved to the REV5's
messages, plus its own deck buttons:

- **KEY LOCK**, **SLIP**, **CENSOR** (SHIFT: reverse) and **KEY − / +**
  (SHIFT: key sync, reset the key).
- **LOOP 1/2X** and **LOOP 2X** halve and double the loop; with SHIFT they
  set loop in and loop out. **AUTO LOOP** starts a beat loop or leaves it.
- Not mapped yet: **STEMS**, the **PIANO PLAY** pad mode, **TEMPO RANGE**,
  the FX unit buttons and the BPM transition buttons.

## When you try it

- Play, scratch and loop on all four decks.
- Try each pad mode, with and without SHIFT.
- Check that the lights follow what's on screen on decks 1-4.
