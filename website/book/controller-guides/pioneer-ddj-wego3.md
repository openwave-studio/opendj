## Getting started

Plug the DDJ-WeGO3 in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by
itself. Choose where the sound goes in **Settings › Audio**.

Around each jog wheel:

- **LOOP** sets a loop and turns it off; **1/2X** and **2X** halve and double
  it. With SHIFT, **1/2X** sets the loop's start, **2X** its end, and
  **LOOP** returns to the loop or leaves it.
- **FX1**, **FX2** and **FX3** turn that side's three effects on and off;
  with SHIFT they pick the next effect.
- Pads **1** to **4** play hot cues, or the deck's four samplers when the
  **HOT CUE/SAMPLER** button is on SAMPLER; SHIFT deletes the hot cue or
  stops the sampler.
- The platter scratches when **Settings › Decks › Vinyl mode** is on for the
  deck; otherwise it bends the speed, as the rim does. SHIFT and the jog
  moves quickly through the track.

On the mixer, **BROWSE** moves through the library and its press moves to the
next pane. SHIFT and **BROWSE** zooms the waveforms.

## When you try it

- Play and scratch on both decks.
- Set a loop with LOOP and change its length.
- Try the pads in both modes, with and without SHIFT.
