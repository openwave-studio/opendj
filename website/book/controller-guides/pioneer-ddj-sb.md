## Getting started

Plug the DDJ-SB in with its USB cable and it picks this mapping by itself.
It's also a sound card: choose it in **Settings › Audio**.

This is Mixxx's mapping. Two decks; the pads have **HOT CUE**, **AUTO
LOOP**, **MANUAL LOOP** and **SAMPLER** modes, with Hot Cue 5-8, Roll, Hot
Loop and Bank on SHIFT. The **FILTER** knobs filter each deck, and the FX
buttons drive openDJ's two effect units.

## When you try it

- Play, scratch and loop on both decks.
- Try every pad mode, with and without SHIFT.
