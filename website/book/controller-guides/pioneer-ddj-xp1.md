## Getting started

The DDJ-XP1 is an add-on: plug it in beside your main controller and it
picks this mapping by itself. It has no sound card.

This is openDJ's own mapping, written from AlphaTheta's MIDI message list
on top of the [DDJ-FLX6's](pioneer-ddj-flx6.md):

- The **DECK** buttons pick the deck each side plays, decks 1-4.
- 16 pads per side: in **HOT CUE** mode the top row plays hot cues 1-8 and
  the bottom row 9-16 (SHIFT clears them). The other pad modes play like
  the FLX6's.
- **LOOP IN**, **LOOP OUT** and **RELOOP/EXIT** set and leave loops.
- **BEAT SYNC**, **LOAD** and **BROWSE** work as on the FLX6.
- Not mapped yet: **SLIDE FX**, **QUANTIZE** and the key buttons.

## When you try it

- Play hot cues 1-16 on all four decks.
- Try each pad mode, with and without SHIFT.
