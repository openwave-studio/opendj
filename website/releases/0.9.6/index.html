openDJ 0.9.6

Another test release on the way to 1.0. A command line and an MCP server let you search, tag and sort your library, analyse tracks, make stems and plan a set from a terminal or an AI assistant, and never while a deck plays. openDJ Studio gets an editable piano roll, MIDI recording, undo and more of Push 2, and instrument decks can play a hardware synth.

Decks

  • Scratch with the on-screen jog: drag its top, or use two fingers on a trackpad.
  • The mixer's EQ / STEMS / SCULPT drop-down works again, with an icon per mode.
  • Instant stems on the Neural Engine on Apple Silicon Macs: about 6× faster.
  • Phone remote Deck tab: an iPad or phone plays any deck over Wi-Fi, with waveform, pads, pitch and channel strip.

Command line and MCP

  • opendj <command>: search, rate, tag and make lists from a terminal; the open app shows the changes.
  • Prep: analyse tracks, make HQ stems and get a health report, all waiting while a deck plays.
  • Set planning: order a list by flow, key or tempo, and get suggestions for the next track.
  • opendj mcp: the same tools for AI assistants over stdio, with no deck controls, no deletes and no network port.

openDJ Studio (preview)

  • An editable piano roll: draw, move and resize notes, velocity and chance lanes, quantize, chop and join, drum mode.
  • Record MIDI into slots, with overdub and Fixed Length, and 64 steps of undo.
  • Track mute and solo, a metronome, and Live's rack macro mappings.
  • Push 2: Device mode, Mute, Solo, Tap Tempo, Duplicate, Double Loop, Record, Fixed Length and Undo.

Instruments and controllers

  • Hardware synths: play a MicroFreak or Moog Messenger from an instrument deck, with named knobs, Note FX, the sequencer, clock sync and presets by bank or category.
  • Instrument deck knob names show in full, with the value beside a knob while you turn it.
  • Arturia presets in the Jam column and on the deck, tagged and with your likes starred.
  • Komplete Kontrol M32 knobs glide smoothly over HID.

Every change, fix and known limit is in the release notes on GitHub.
