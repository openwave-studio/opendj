Test release Released 26 November 2026

openDJ 0.9.5

A test release on the way to 1.0. A tidier top bar and mixer, a phase meter for beatmatching by ear, a simpler instrument deck with a plugin panel, a one-column Jam source, a first Session preview that plays Ableton Live sets in time with your decks, Pure Data effects and instruments, HQ stems that start at the playhead, smoother waveforms, Windows audio fixes, and Reloop, DDJ-1000 and Komplete Kontrol M32 mappings.

Decks

  • A tidier top bar: one pill style, a Master pill with wider meters, the clock in its own pill, and a compact WAVE panel.
  • A phase meter under each 2-Deck overview: a big beat number, the countdown to the next phrase, EARLY / LATE against the master deck and tempo arrows.
  • The 2-Deck mixer has meters beside the faders, and a Deck options pill under each key.
  • Pad tabs: four tabs over the pad columns again, with each page's setting under the pads.
  • A slimmer FX bar in two rows, with Beat FX in its own box and one Release button.
  • A grid editor bar under the decks in 2-Deck: grid type, BPM, downbeat, anchors, nudges and Clear.
  • Play starts on the press, right on the beat.
  • Pitch Color FX: the COLOR knob shifts a deck up to an octave down or up.

Instruments, Jam and Session

  • The instrument deck: PLAY or SEQ, eight bigger knobs, a scope with wave or spectrum, and Note FX tiles for Scale, Chord and Arp, with strum, swing and new scales.
  • The plugin panel shows the instrument in the browser area. OPEN on the deck opens it.
  • Jam is one column with Presets and Instruments, search, stars and filter chips.
  • Session (preview): File › Open Live Set… opens an Ableton Live set, and its clips launch from the Jam source in time with your decks.
  • Pure Data patches as effects and instruments, with cyclone, ELSE and pd-lua displays (experimental).

Stems

  • HQ stems are ready around the playhead first, and a whole crate can be prepared at once.
  • The HQ cache takes a quarter less space.
  • File › Export › Stem Files… writes NI STEM files.
  • Stems on tracks not at 44.1 kHz stay in line with the track.

Library

  • A Background ring and panel for analysis, HQ stems, lyrics and covers, with pause, stop and Do next.
  • Analysis runs several tracks at once while no deck plays, and finds lyrics as it goes.
  • rekordbox import finds beat grids in more libraries, and USB export fixes for Pioneer players.

Waveforms

  • Waveforms and the jog marker follow the display's refresh and no longer shake.
  • The overview waveform shows true loudness.

Controllers

  • Reloop: the whole current line and several older models, with pictures and guides.
  • Pioneer DJ DDJ-1000 with its jog displays, and DDJ-FLX10 fixes.
  • Komplete Kontrol M32 drives the instrument deck.
  • All new mappings are Beta. If you have one, tell us how it plays.

Windows and fixes

  • ASIO plays as the master output, devices are scanned once, and a missing master falls back to the default device.
  • Collection Health no longer hangs on large libraries.
  • Plugin helper crash fixes on macOS.

Every change, fix and known limit is in the release notes on GitHub.
