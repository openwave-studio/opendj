openDJ 0.8

From the booth to the studio. Open a recorded set as a timeline, check it, fix it and export it with chapters. Collection Health is rebuilt, Beat FX work the same on every controller, the instrument deck gets a step sequencer, and Room EQ tunes your outputs to the venue.

Mixed Set Editor

  • Right-click a recording or a History session and choose Open in Mixed Set Editor: the set as track blocks over its waveform, with a row per deck.
  • Step through every transition with its length, style and whether the beats stayed in time.
  • Check my mix finds clipping, silences, level jumps and drifting beats.
  • Trim, cut, fix levels and set loudness, then export a Mixcloud tracklist, YouTube chapters or a cue sheet.
  • Save the transitions you liked as pair mixes, so Automix mixes those tracks the same way next time.

Collection Health

  • One row per check, grouped under Files, Location, Duplicates, Metadata, Prep and Organization.
  • The Overview explains the score: which checks hold it back, and by how much.
  • Duplicates found by sound, even with different or missing tags, grouped as Sure, Likely or Versions.
  • New Tags and Lists pages, and Quarantine with Bring back.

Beat FX

  • Beat FX work the same on every controller, stepping through effects in list order.
  • When an effect changes from a controller, its name glows and a strip shows the effects either side.
  • MST sends the effect to the main mix. Echoes and rolls go up to 16 beats.

Instruments

  • A redesigned instrument deck: a slim title, preset heart, bigger knobs in the instrument's color, and Scale, Chord and Arp pills.
  • A step sequencer with pitch, octave, velocity, gate and chance lanes, four pattern slots, REC from keys and pads, and Dice for a new riff.
  • HOLD for any instrument.

Room EQ

  • Six bands on the main and booth outputs, with presets for common rooms.
  • A live spectrum with Before and After. Click a peak to cut it.
  • Bypass and A/B to hear the difference. Recordings are taken before Room EQ.

Library and DJing

  • Played marks: tracks played in this session are dimmed with a lilac check.
  • TIDAL catalog search, with a dropdown as you type.
  • Jog wheels bend like Mixxx mappings, and touching a platter no longer clicks.
  • Karaoke shows the cover beside the lyrics, full screen.
  • Steadier tap tempo, and the MASTER clock shows the time of day.

Controllers

  • Pioneer OPUS-QUAD (Beta).
  • DDJ-FLX10: jogs scaled for its big platters, pad colors that match the screen, and tempo and key on the jog displays.
  • DDJ-400: Beat FX, rim sensitivity, Slip release and the SHIFT pad modes work.

Fixed in 0.8.1

  • Windows starts again: 0.8.0 closed before its window opened.
  • High Quality stems work in the installed app.
  • RGB waveforms get new colors, true loudness up close, and the real wave at the closest zoom.

Every change, fix and known limit is in the release notes on GitHub: 0.8.0 · 0.8.1.
