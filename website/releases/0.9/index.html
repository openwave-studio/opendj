openDJ 0.9

Ready for the club. A new look runs across the whole app, Remix FX put effects on the lows, mids or highs and add a Remix deck with scenes and rolling drum pads, and the Mixed Set Editor can mix a transition again or swap a track. Browse from the keyboard, fit a small laptop screen, and plug in nearly 70 more controllers.

A new look

  • One set of controls everywhere: switch pills that light lilac, ‹ value › steppers, titled menus, power buttons and arc knobs.
  • Play lights green and CUE yellow. The jog is a thin ring with the time inside, and every fader is a thin lilac track.
  • In 2-Deck the jog and pads sit side by side, and the tempo column has SYNC at the bottom.
  • A master knob with tall meters. Right-click it for the Master panel.
  • Deck letters M B K S P show and switch tempo leader, match tempo and key on load, sync on play and key lock.

Remix FX

  • Band FX: any effect, built-in or plugin, can play on only the lows, mids or highs.
  • Release lets go of every effect at once: Echo, Brake, Spin or Cut.
  • New effects: Tape Echo, Ducker, Rhythm and Solo.
  • The Remix deck plays on top of another deck, the mic or an aux input. It has an ISOLATE knob and a LEVER per band, a SCENE wheel with five Build Ups and five Break Downs, and X-PAD drum pads that roll.

Mixed Set Editor

  • Mix it again: give a transition a new length in bars and a new curve (Smooth, Bass swap, Filter or Cut), and hear it right away.
  • Swap a track for another from your library, best tempo and key matches first. It comes in on the same bar.
  • A band-colored waveform, a Tracks tab, and long sets that play straight from disk.

Library

  • Keyboard browsing: arrows and Tab move through the rail, folders and tracks. Shift+← and Shift+→ load decks 1 and 2, and the BROWSE knob does the same.
  • Small screens: on laptop screens the lists and side panels slide over the track list, and there's an Interface scale and List text size.
  • Shortlist and Playlist tabs in the side panel. New lists go first, and several lists move together.
  • TIDAL: BPM and key before you download, progress on the row, and artist pages in the search dropdown.

Waveforms and grids

  • Grid modes per track: Auto, Fixed, or Flexible to follow a live drummer bar by bar.
  • BPM labels on the waveform where the tempo changes, joined to phrase flags (DROP · 126).

Controllers

  • Pioneer DJ: DDJ-GRV6 with GROOVE CIRCUIT, DDJ-FLX2, REV5, XP1, XP2, SR, SP1 and older DDJs, the XDJ all-in-ones, DJM mixers and the PLX-CRSS12. Waveforms on the DDJ-FLX10's jog screens.
  • Native Instruments: Traktor Kontrol X1 MK3, Z1 MK2, D2, S5 and S8, and the S2 MK3, S3, S4 MK3 and MX2 play as Traktor does.
  • Numark, Hercules and Denon DJ: new mappings and pictures drawn from the makers' manuals.
  • All new mappings are Beta. If you have one, tell us how it plays.

Fixes

  • Everything from 0.8.1: Windows starts, High Quality stems work in the installed app, and the new RGB waveforms.
  • Buttons that controller scripts press again and again (SYNC, loop halve and double) work every time.
  • Pitch faders sent as pitch bend work in every mapping.

Every change, fix and known limit is in the release notes on GitHub.
