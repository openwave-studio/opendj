## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Sound Color FX PITCH on Pioneer controllers
On Pioneer mappings whose controller has a SOUND COLOR FX PITCH button (DDJ-FLX10, XDJ-R1, XDJ-RR, DDJ-RX, DDJ-RZ, DDJ-RZX, DDJ-SZ, DDJ-800), the button SHALL make Pitch the Color FX of every deck and light; pressing it again while lit SHALL go back to the filter. The DDJ-RX, RZ, RZX, SZ and DDJ-800 mappings SHALL map their other Sound Color FX buttons the same way where openDJ has a matching Color FX (SWEEP plays the filter).

#### Scenario: PITCH on a DDJ-FLX10
- **WHEN** PITCH is pressed on a DDJ-FLX10
- **THEN** the PITCH button lights and every deck's COLOR knob shifts the pitch
